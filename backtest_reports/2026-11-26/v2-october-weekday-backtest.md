# V2 October weekday backtest

This replay covers 2–31 October 2026 (IST), the final 30 calendar days before November. There were 21 eligible weekdays. The last scored result was Friday, 30 October.

For every target date, V2 1.0.29 used the app’s usual prior history and withheld both actual results until scoring. Earlier chart results could inform the forecast, including published weekend draws. Only Monday–Friday targets contributed to accuracy.

An exact panel match anywhere in 30 SP plus 20 DP counted as a panel hit. An actual Sutta digit appearing in the first four V2 Sutta picks counted as a Sutta hit. The same completed draws were scored for Open and Close, with both forecasts made before Open.
