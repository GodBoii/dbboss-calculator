# Two-year walk-forward study

Scope is fixed to 2024-10-09 through 2026-10-08, based on the user's date of 2026-10-09 in Asia/Kolkata. No older outcome may enter a model, pooled prior, feature, selection statistic, or saved source snapshot. Today's partial results are excluded.

Each prediction uses strictly earlier dates. Close-after-Open predictions additionally receive only the target's Open panel. They are scored separately from pre-Open Close. All models return six Sutta digits and ten exact panels. Absent-pair success requires both digits to be absent. TP is distinct from DP.

The first 180 complete same-market draws are warmup, not scored targets. Development ends 2025-10-08. Selection is 2025-10-09 through 2026-04-08. Confirmation is 2026-04-09 through 2026-10-08. Choose one global candidate per task on selection accuracy, except DP where selection minimizes Brier loss. No confirmation result may choose parameters or routes. Recent 30/90/365-day windows and market breakdowns are descriptive only. Date-clustered paired bootstrap intervals compare each frozen winner with the same-event production reference.

Candidate families are fixed before running: full-window frequency, recent 30/90/180-draw frequency, 45-draw exponential decay, pooled shrinkage with 50 pseudo-draws, weekday conditioning with 20 pseudo-draws, and Sutta-transition conditioning with 20 pseudo-draws. Candidate distributions have a uniform one-draw prior over the 220 legal panels. Conditional models shrink toward same-market frequencies. Every candidate is refit from permitted earlier outcomes at each target. Sutta and absent-pair rankings marginalize the candidate's panel distribution. Close-after-Open conditions on earlier Open-to-Close transitions.

Production rankings are a fixed current-code reference. They include previously selected market rules, historical calibration constants, and panel weights fitted through 2026-07-02. Their historical training cannot be undone by truncating runtime inputs. They are not an as-deployed historical simulation, and this study does not assert that production was trained only on these two years. Challenger training is strictly limited to this study's window. Production panel comparisons after 2026-07-02 are reported separately. Existing research has inspected overlapping dates; confirmation here is retrospective, not blind prospective evidence.

Record actual source coverage, excluded incomplete/invalid rows, snapshot hash, code hash, model versions, permitted-history boundaries, actual results, and every model's predictions. Fetch via the app's chart validation and supplementary-source pipeline. Missing results are not fabricated and incomplete market-days do not count as complete draws. Chart schedule times cannot establish historical publication timestamps, so cross-market same-day inputs are excluded.

No automatic production promotion. A candidate can be recommended only if the frozen confirmation comparison is positive with its 95% date-clustered interval above zero, has enough observations, and does not depend solely on one market or a short recent streak. Reusing a confirmation block after observing it must be labeled development in subsequent iterations.
